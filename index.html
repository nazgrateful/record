Frequency Recorder

Record your screen with its internal audio and/or your microphone, with background noise removed. When a frequency you've named shows up in the audio, the app names it on screen, logs the time, and can mute it.

5. Saved recordings

Recordings are saved on this website automatically, written every second while you record. If the tab crashes or closes, the recording is recovered the next time you open the page. Download them to keep copies on your computer. Each one also has subtitle files (.vtt / .srt) of the names detected.
