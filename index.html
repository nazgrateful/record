5. Saved recordings

Recordings are saved on this website automatically, written every second while you record. If the tab crashes or closes, the recording is recovered the next time you open the page. They're also downloaded automatically when you stop (if that's turned on), and can be written straight into a folder you choose, such as your Desktop. Each one also has subtitle files (.vtt / .srt) of the names detected.

Updates to this website never remove your recordings. A backup folder also protects them if the browser's data gets cleared or you switch computers: backing up again only copies new recordings, and restoring only adds missing ones.

Imported audio is kept in this website's storage, so it's still here if you reload. 12 hours of sound is about 0.4 GB as Opus, 0.7 GB as MP3 at 128 kbps or 8 GB as stereo WAV. Converting to MP3 takes about 5 minutes per hour of stereo sound (so about an hour for 12 hours); Opus and WAV are much faster.
